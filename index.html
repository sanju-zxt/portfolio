Who I am

I'm a results-driven full-stack developer and data engineer in the making, studying BE in Computer Science & Engineering (Data Science) at Vijaya Vittala Institute of Technology. Algorithms, machine learning and statistics aren't just coursework for me — they're the tools I reach for when a project needs to be measurably better than the obvious version.

I build scalable web apps, AI-powered systems and automation that people actually use: a propensity model with a proven 2.5× lift, a four-week hackathon platform with 52 passing tests, and a campus app in production. I care about the unglamorous parts — config over hard-coding, tests, CI, Docker, honest metrics.

In November 2026 I was selected as an AWS Student Builder Group Leader for VVIT — one of a global community of student leaders across 68+ countries. I'm building the campus AWS developer community, running cloud workshops and cert-prep sessions, and mentoring a Core Team so it outlives my term.

Off the clock you'll find me reading framework changelogs, shipping side projects, or writing about what broke and why. My goal is simple: close the gap between data-driven insight and a user experience that feels effortless.
